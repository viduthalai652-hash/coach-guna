Problem Solver
A common startup mistake is to build a solution before clearly identifying the problem. Products created this way may fail to address a genuine customer need.
02
Market Research
Market research is essential. Inadequate research can lead a business to target the wrong audience or misunderstand the market in which it intends to compete.
03
Target Market
A startup cannot sell effectively to everyone. It must identify the people most likely to need, value, and purchase its offer.
04
Business Model
Innovation alone is insufficient. A startup requires a clear, sustainable business model to survive and grow in a highly competitive environment.
